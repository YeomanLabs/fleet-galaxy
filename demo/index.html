    Fleet Galaxy

    An Intune-managed Windows fleet drawn as a galaxy. Each galaxy is a group (a site, ring, model or release) and each star is one device.

    What you're looking at

    • Distance from the core is recency: device check-in, or a person's last sign-in. Anything silent for a week or more drifts past the disc edge into the halo.
    • Color is the lens you pick: compliance, patching, deployments, Defender, Endpoint Analytics, lifecycle, or any custom field. Press L for all of them.
    • Bigger stars need attention. Dim ones have no data.
    • People (P) switches to users: MFA strength, sign-in activity, risk and licenses. Click a person to see their devices, and draw lines between them in the device galaxy.
    • The timeline appears when you load several snapshots. Play it to watch the fleet change day by day.
